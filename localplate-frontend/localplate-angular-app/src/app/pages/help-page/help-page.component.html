Help & FAQ

How do I find nearby food shops?

Open the Find Food page and use the map to explore listed shops and coordinates.

Can I suggest a new shop?

Yes. Use the Feedback page and share the shop name, location, and notes.

Who can use CRUD dashboard?

Only authenticated users can access dashboard features and edit shop data.

Can I access public pages after login?

Current routing keeps logged-in users focused on the dashboard. After logout, public pages are available again.

Do you support new neighborhood requests?

Yes. Share area details in feedback and we can include additional local mapping in upcoming updates.

Will pricing data be live?

This version is for test/demo CRUD. Real-time updates can be added with verified vendor data integrations.
